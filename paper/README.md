# Predictive Mirror paper

- [Read the paper (PDF)](From_Pixels_to_Persistent_Worlds.pdf)
- [Editable manuscript (Markdown)](From_Pixels_to_Persistent_Worlds.md)

The PDF is a **six-part illustrated research note** with conceptual vector figures. It does **not** fabricate model-output galleries. Where checkpoint galleries have not been packaged with the paper, figure panels indicate the measurement that belongs there before a research release.

The experimental MSE figures are marked as **operator-reported historical measurements**, not newly reproduced outcomes. The Markdown is the canonical text. Future figure-production PRs can replace the conceptual panels with artifact-linked real output and generate a new PDF.

Research goals are ordered around reproducing strong reference capabilities, improving model performance and then studying causal mechanisms. Citation and methodology detail lives in `docs/` and the independent experiment ledger.
